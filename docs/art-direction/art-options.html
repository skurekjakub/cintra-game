The Lion's Shadow · real-time RPG · Godot 4.7 · art direction, round 1

Five looks for one bad night at the Drowned Rat

Every option below draws the same moment. The royal courier lies dead by the table at the Drowned Rat, the dockside tavern in Cintra, and three hired Choir thugs have just kicked in the door to take his letter. Bertha is behind the counter. You stand between the thugs and the letter.

Each mockup is drawn live in this page with canvas code: geometry, light falloff, hatching, dither and noise. No painted or generated images were used, which is also the limit the game's asset pipeline will work under. Feasibility is rated for that pipeline: Blender Python, Godot shaders, and CC0 libraries (Poly Haven, Kenney, Quaternius). Pick one by number and say which in chat.

Option 1 · low-poly 3D · isometric

Lantern Diorama

The world as a lit model on a table. Faceted wood and stone, every hearth and candle baking a warm pool into a cold blue night, the edges of the frame softened like a macro photograph.

Direction

Camera
Fixed 3/4 isometric (orthographic, about 35° pitch), rotates in 90° steps. Roofs and near walls cut away indoors.
Lighting
Baked vertex colour and lightmaps from hearths, candles and lanterns; one cold blue moon; tilt-shift depth of field.
Mood
storybook menacecosy dreadlegiblehandmade

Trade-offs

Pros

  • Fights read at a glance from the high camera.
  • Crude geometry looks intentional.
  • Quaternius and Kenney low-poly packs drop straight in.
  • Cheap to light, cheap to run, scales to towns and roads.

Cons

  • The toy scale can soften cruelty and grief.
  • Faces are tiny; dialogue needs close-up portraits.
  • Low-poly isometric is a crowded indie look without strict lighting discipline.

Production feasibility

High

Faceted boxes and baked vertex colour are exactly what Blender Python and the CC0 low-poly packs produce, and there are no textures to author.

  • Blender Python: modular kit (walls, floors, props) on a 0.5 m grid, flat normals, vertex-colour bake.
  • Godot: orthographic Camera3D, LightmapGI, a tilt-shift CompositorEffect.
  • CC0: Quaternius characters and Kenney props, recoloured to the palette.

Option 2 · 3D with an engraving shader

Woodcut Chronicle

The kingdom's own chronicle come alive. Every surface is cut in swelling ink lines, light is bare paper, and blood and fire are the only red.

The dead man's letter. Hand it over and nobody else gets wet.IM Fell English italic · dialogue and captions, a 17th-century press face

Direction

Camera
Third-person, high 3/4 chase camera with slow framing. Dialogue cuts to flat, side-on "plate" compositions like a printed illustration.
Lighting
Light is the absence of ink. A screen-space shader turns luminance into hatch density and line width, cross-hatches the shadows, and inks outlines from depth and normal edges.
Mood
austerefatalisticdocumentarycruel

Trade-offs

Pros

  • Instantly distinctive.
  • The shader carries the look, so assets can be plain grey geometry.
  • Red becomes a gameplay signal: blood, fire, danger.
  • Fits a chronicle of a doomed reign.

Cons

  • Busy fights can turn to line noise without careful tuning.
  • Hatching shimmers in motion; needs temporal stabilising.
  • Needs a long shader iteration loop to get right.

Production feasibility

High–medium

Geometry can stay crude because the hatching shader overrides surface detail; the risk is shader tuning and temporal stability, not asset count.

  • Blender Python: untextured blockout meshes with clean normals; a vertex-colour "ink ID" for line control.
  • Godot: full-screen post shader (depth+normal edge detection, luminance-to-hatch), a mask pass for the red objects.
  • CC0: Quaternius rigs and animations; no textures needed.

Direction

Camera
Close, low over-the-shoulder third person with modern controls (no tank steering).
Lighting
Per-vertex light from a few point lights, heavy teal-black distance fog, emissive fires that punch through it. 320×200 internal render.
Mood
claustrophobicuncannycoldoppressive

Trade-offs

Pros

  • Crude agent-made assets read as a stylistic choice.
  • 64–128 px textures can be generated from noise.
  • Fog hides draw distance and empty space.
  • Strong horror for the Ostrel vaults and the night roads.

Cons

  • Retro is a crowded niche and can read as pastiche.
  • Low resolution makes faces and in-world text hard.
  • The court-intrigue half of the story may feel thin.

Production feasibility

Very high

Low fidelity hides crude meshes, textures can be tiny procedural noise, and one post shader (resolution, dither, quantise) does most of the work.

  • Blender Python: meshes under 1k triangles; 64–128 px textures baked from noise nodes.
  • Godot: SubViewport at 320×200, vertex-snap spatial shader, Environment fog, Bayer dither post shader.
  • CC0: Quaternius and Kenney models; Poly Haven textures downscaled to 128 px.

Direction

Camera
Third-person over the shoulder at eye height, shallow depth of field, slow lens moves.
Lighting
Physically based: Poly Haven overcast and night HDRIs, volumetric fog with light shafts, warm practical lights against cold moonlight, SDFGI.
Mood
bleakheavyintimatesober

Trade-offs

Pros

  • Highest emotional ceiling for cruelty and grief.
  • Poly Haven scans give real material quality for free.
  • Fog and light in Godot 4 can look superb in environments.

Cons

  • Realistic faces, cloth and animation are hard without hand art and fall into the uncanny.
  • Volumetrics cost performance.
  • Every crude asset stands out against scanned materials.

Production feasibility

Medium

Scanned CC0 materials and HDRIs make environments achievable; believable realistic characters and facial animation are the hard part for an agent pipeline.

  • Blender Python: architecture kit with Poly Haven displacement; characters from CC0 base meshes, with hoods and helmets hiding faces.
  • Godot: SDFGI or LightmapGI, volumetric fog, TAA, depth of field.
  • CC0: Poly Haven textures, models and HDRIs; Quaternius rigs retargeted.

Direction

Camera
2D top-down plan with upright figures (medieval map logic). Locations turn like pages; the realm map is a folio.
Lighting
None in the physical sense. Flat colour; gold leaf catches a moving sheen; night is deeper ultramarine and candle haloes.
Mood
devotionalironicstorybookominous marginalia

Trade-offs

Pros

  • Unique, and thematically exact: the Book of the Lion and its sliced verse become the interface.
  • 2D is cheap to render.
  • Marginalia UI is a memorable hook.

Cons

  • Animation without image generation is hard: every figure must be a cut-out puppet built in code.
  • Real-time combat readability with flat top-down figures is unproven.
  • Scale and atmosphere (blizzards, vaults) are harder to sell.

Production feasibility

Medium–low

Vector cut-out puppets can work, but every figure, gesture and creature has to be authored as shapes in code with no image generation to lean on.

  • Scripts: procedural SVG and Polygon2D figure parts; diaper-pattern floor tiles.
  • Godot: Skeleton2D cut-out rigs, gold-leaf sheen and vellum shaders, TileMap floors.
  • Blender / CC0: barely used; no 3D library fits this look.
