# THE LION'S SHADOW
### A Low-Magic Dark Fantasy Campaign in Cintra (1268)

* **System:** System-agnostic (calibrated for d20/OSR/gritty fantasy, Levels 1–12 across three acts)
* **Length:** 25–35 sessions, plus modular side quests
* **Tone:** Muddy roads, dynastic succession crisis, border feuds, cold dwarven engineering, and human cruelty—where "monsters" are either natural predators driven out of their territory or weapons left behind by desperate men.
* **Calendar:** Autumn Equinox (Sept 21, 1268) to Winter Solstice Dawn (Dec 21, 1268) — **90 Days**.

---

## 1. The Premise & The Core Truth

Four hundred years ago, **Corvan the Lion** forged the Kingdom of Cintra with the aid of the dwarves of **Karak Ostrel**. To break his rivals, Corvan used ancient dwarven siege-engines, alchemical blight-casks, and fanatical blood-sworn shock troops forged in the deep vaults beneath the Góry Amell. Recognizing that such weapons would eventually destroy the kingdom they built, Corvan and the last High King of Ostrel sealed the **Deep-Vaults of Karak Ostrel** behind heavy rune-forged bulkheads and pressure locks.

Every **19 years** at winter solstice dawn, the reigning monarch of Cintra—wearing the physical **Crown of the Lion**—must speak the **Four Verses of the Pact** at the **Ring of Nine** (the surface relay outside the capital). The ritual vents subterranean pressure and resets the mechanical/alchemical deadbolts for another cycle.

### The Crisis of 1268
* **The Dying Queen:** Queen Ysolde (40) is wasting away from slow **grey lily** poisoning—a rare mountain neurotoxin that mimics a wasting fever.
* **The Uncrowned Heir:** Prince Aldric (12) has the royal bloodline, but without the physical Crown and formal designation as Regent/Co-Sovereign, the lock mechanisms will not recognize his voice.
* **The Saboteur:** **Abbot Anselm** of St. Corwen—the Queen’s trusted confessor and a bitter former military chaplain from the 1263 Marnadal war—believes Cintra’s nobility is irredeemably corrupt. Leading a radical cell called **the Pale Choir**, Anselm has poisoned the Queen, sliced the fourth verse out of the Abbey’s *Book of the Lion*, and plans to abduct Aldric. Spoken *in reverse* by royal blood inside Karak Ostrel’s control chamber, the Pact does not lock the vaults—it blows the seals open.

### The Hook
Late September 1268. A dying royal courier stumbles into **The Drowned Rat**, a dockside tavern in Cintra, foaming at the mouth from grey lily toxin. He clutches a dispatch sealed in dwarven iron-wax from **Dravin Ostrel**: *"The lower bolts in Ostrel are grinding loose. Something old is waking in the dark. Come before the winter lock fails."* Before he dies, he gasps one word: *"Ostrel."* Within the hour, three hired Choir thugs kick open the tavern door to recover the letter.

## 2. The Realm in 1268

Cintra is wealthy, proud, and fracturing along old fault lines.

| Region | Seat | Political & Economic Reality |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cintra (Crown Lands)** | Cintra (Yaruga mouth) | Rich in grain, wool, and river tolls. The court is paralyzed by a cold war between **Lord Marshal Kosk** and **Chancellor Varn** over who will seize the regency. |
| **Duchy of Attre** | Attre (Free Port) | Semi-autonomous coastal power. **Duke Raimund** smuggles contraband through Port Peixe and plans to declare full independence the moment Ysolde dies. |
| **Strept Highlands** | Dun Strept | Fierce highland clans in the Amell foothills. Still nursing blood-hatred over the **Battle of Marnadal (1263)**, where Cintran heavy horse burned three clan halls. |
| **Góry Amell** | *None (Karak Ostrel)* | Impassable in deep winter. Karak Ostrel has been sealed for a century; only treacherous goat tracks and the **Marnadal Stairs** reach the high passes. |
| **Sodden (North-East)** | Breza (Border Town) | Rival kingdom of fens and timber-lords. Sodden’s spymasters are quietly bribing Cintran border barons and waiting for civil war. |
| **Brugge & Verden** | Kernow / Vidort | Paper allies to the north. Their envoys linger in Cintra’s court, stalling treaties until they see who holds power in spring. |
| **Mag Turga (South-East)** | Assengard | Pine-forested marches. Home to **Dravin Ostrel**, the last surviving smith of Karak Ostrel’s gate-warden line. |

### Recent History (Player-Discoverable Timeline)
* **1249:** Queen Ysolde (then 21) speaks the Oath at the Ring of Nine without incident.
* **1262:** The Crown commissions a new realm survey—the exact map the players carry.
* **1263:** **Battle of Marnadal.** A Choir provocateur fans border tensions into open war; Lord Marshal Kosk crushes the Strept clans and burns three ancestral halls to force a surrender.
* **Spring 1268:** Ysolde falls ill. The Abbey bells ring unbidden on the equinox night (Anselm testing signals and rattling court nerves).
* **Autumn 1268:** A mountain wyvern is spotted over the Amell foothills for the first time in living memory; livestock vanish near Tigg.

## 3. Factions & Key NPCs

Every major figure at court has a dirty secret, ensuring players must investigate carefully rather than rely on first impressions.

### Court & Crown
* **Queen Ysolde (40):** Sharp-minded despite agonizing nerve pain. She knows the truth about Karak Ostrel’s vaults and has confided only in Anselm. She hides her decline behind heavy velvet robes to keep vultures from circling her son.
* **Prince Aldric (12):** Proud, observant, and frustrated by being treated like a fragile porcelain doll. He has overheard Anselm speaking bitter truths about court corruption, making him vulnerable to manipulation, though his loyalty to his mother is fierce.
* **Lord Marshal Bertran Kosk:** Grizzled, pragmatic soldier who wants order and Aldric safely crowned. **Secret:** He ordered the burning of the Strept halls in 1263 to end a bloody guerrilla campaign and is deeply haunted by it. The clans will spit on any envoy wearing his badge.
* **Chancellor Orsik Varn:** Pompous, sweating, and aggressively lobbying for the regency. **The Red Herring:** Varn has embezzled thousands of crowns from Yaruga river tolls over the last decade. He sneaks around at night burning incriminating ledgers—acting like a guilty assassin when he is merely a common thief.

### The Conspirators & Power Brokers
* **Abbot Anselm (St. Corwen):** Grandfatherly, soft-spoken scholar and the Queen’s confessor. **The Villain:** Served as an army chaplain at Marnadal in 1263 and grew disgusted by how Cintran nobles slaughtered peasants and pocketed war indemnities. He poisons Ysolde’s communion wine with grey lily and leads the Pale Choir to unseal Ostrel’s vaults and burn the old order down.
* **Duke Raimund of Attre:** Charming, ruthless merchant-prince. Wants Attre free of Cintran tariffs. **Secret:** Smuggles contraband through Port Peixe and Attre Light; he sold grey lily bulbs to Choir agents for heavy silver without caring what they were for.
* **Sister Maelis (St. Corwen):** Young, observant archivist who noticed the freshly sliced pages in the *Book of the Lion* and realized Anselm’s sermons contradict older records. A crucial ally—or the Choir’s next murder victim.

### Borderlands & Wilds
* **Old Wenna (Witch’s Mere):** Prickly, transactional hedge-witch who wants to be left alone. Grows the only cultivated grey lily patch in the realm; Choir raiders stole her harvest. She knows the exact counter-dose if given back her bulbs.
* **"Ninefingers" Jory (Ferrymen’s Camp):** Pragmatic river-bandit kingpin controlling the Marnadal crossings. Ferries humming iron crates and hooded monks by night because their silver is good—and will sell them out just as fast if paid better or cornered.
* **Hesk Ironjaw (Dun Strept):** Scarred highland chieftain demanding justice and blood-price for 1263. His clan guards an ancient standing stone carved with the original four verses of the Pact.
* **Dravin Ostrel (Assengard):** Stubborn, aging dwarf blacksmith. Holds the heavy **Iron-Key** to Karak Ostrel’s gate, though the inner lock still requires the Crown and a royal voice.

### The Pale Choir
Roughly 35–40 members: radicalized monks, bribed palace servants, the royal food-taster, and hired cutthroats. They wear a **pale swan or gull feather** stitched inside a collar or boot-lining. They pass orders via a hollow trunk at **Hanged Man’s Oak** and muster at the **Ruins of Vel Aram**.

## 5. Act I: Ashes in the Lion's Den (Levels 1–4)

*Objective: Survive the opening ambush, uncover the poisoning and the missing verse, protect Prince Aldric at the Harvest Fair, and follow the smuggling trail south.*

1. **The Drowned Rat:** The courier dies with Dravin’s letter. Choir thugs arrive within an hour; fight or flee across Cintra’s tidal mudflats. *(Note: Court advisors confirm the mountain passes are already snowed in and Ostrel’s gate won't open without solving the crisis in Cintra first.)*
2. **Audience at Court:** Kosk discreetly hires the party—he cannot trust palace guards on Varn’s payroll. Varn protests loudly; Anselm warmly offers the Abbey’s library and prayers.
3. **The Sickroom & The Red Herring:** Investigation reveals grey lily pollen residue in the Queen’s silver chalice, a royal food-taster suddenly flashing gold coins, and Chancellor Varn burning tax ledgers in a brazier at midnight (a tense chase proving he’s embezzling tolls, not poisoning the Queen).
4. **Abbey of St. Corwen:** Sister Maelis shows the party the *Book of the Lion*—the final verse has been cleanly sliced out with a knife. Anselm claims he discovered the vandalism days ago and provides a **falsified fourth verse** "from memory" to mislead them. Maelis whispers that only Anselm holds the vault key.
5. **The Ring of Nine:** At night outside the walls, one monolith is split and weeping black alchemical tar. Feral hounds driven mad by the smell attack. Comparing the stone’s worn chiseling to Anselm’s "copy" exposes his lie.
6. **Gull Tower Goes Dark (Optional Link):** The coastal watchtower signal goes out. Attre smugglers murdered the keeper; crates in the cellar hold grey lily bulbs, tying the poison directly to Duke Raimund’s ports.
7. **Climax — The Chociebuż Harvest Fair:** Prince Aldric insists on riding through the fair to project strength. Choir assassins spark a grease-fire in the crowded stalls and strike. Saving the boy yields a captured assassin bearing a **pale feather** and a route map pointing to **Hanged Man’s Oak**.

**Act I Transition:** Kosk dispatches the party south along the Marnadal road to secure the antidote, the true fourth verse, and Dravin Ostrel. **Prince Aldric remains in Cintra** under heavy guard, ensuring the party has built personal loyalty with him before Act II’s twist.

## 6. Act II: The Marnadal Road (Levels 5–8)

*Objective: Sandbox investigation across the southern marches. Players can tackle these locations in any order, needing three things before Act III: **Wenna’s antidote**, **proof of Anselm’s treason**, and **the true fourth verse**.*

* **Tigg & The Crooked Crossroads:** Sheep and cattle vanish nightly. The culprit is a **mountain wyvern** driven down from the high crags by subterranean tremors and Choir blasting near Ostrel. Villagers also report hooded monks traveling south after dark.
* **Hanged Man’s Oak:** The Choir’s dead-drop. Ambush a courier or plant a forged dispatch to capture **Anselm’s cipher** and learn the Choir is gathering at **Vel Aram** before marching on the mountains.
* **Ferrymen’s Camp:** Ninefingers Jory controls the river barges. Bribe, intimidate, or cut a deal with him; he admits hauling heavy ironbound crates for St. Corwen and points toward Vel Aram.
* **Battle of Marnadal Field (1263):** Muddy, bone-strewn killing ground. On new-moon nights, bitter revenants reenact the cavalry charge. A fallen clan champion’s spirit asks the party to carry his **bronze-and-iron torc** home to Dun Strept—the key to winning clan support in Act III.
* **Witch’s Mere:** Old Wenna refuses to help Cintra until the party recovers her stolen grey lily bulbs from Attre. Once returned, she brews the **antidote** (which can be sent ahead by fast rider or carried back to Cintra).
* **Attre (Masquerade & Warehouse Raid):** Infiltrate Duke Raimund’s harbor stronghold. Steal his **private shipping ledger** proving Abbey silver paid for the grey lily shipment, recover Wenna’s bulbs, and optionally blackmail/ally with Raimund (who wants stability for trade, not Choir fanatics).
* **Ruins of Vel Aram (Dungeon):** A crumbling pre-Cintran border fortress converted into a Choir sanctum.
  * *Hazards:* Collapse traps, fanatical Choir acolytes, and a **chained soot-hound / dwarven war-construct** hauled out of Ostrel’s outer tunnels.
  * *Treasure:* **Anselm’s private journal** and the **authentic fourth verse**—revealing that speaking the Pact *backward* at Karak Ostrel’s core control throne disengages the vault locks instead of resetting them.

### The Act II Twist
Upon returning to Cintra with the antidote and proof:
* **If Ysolde receives the antidote in time:** She survives, frail and bedridden, and formally invests **Prince Aldric as Co-Sovereign with the Lion’s Crown**, granting him the legal and ceremonial authority to speak the Oath.
* **The Abduction:** **Abbot Anselm has vanished—along with Prince Aldric.** Bribed palace servants slipped the boy out through the Abbey’s catacombs during the night. Anselm is riding hard for Karak Ostrel to force Aldric to speak the reversed Oath at solstice dawn.

## 7. Act III: Under Amell (Levels 9–12)

*Objective: Race winter blizzards into the Góry Amell, rally mountain allies, breach Karak Ostrel, and stop the reversed ritual at solstice dawn.*

1. **The Race South:** Anselm holds a three-day lead up the **Marnadal Stairs**. Players can take the direct ascent (faster, grueling cold and Choir ambushes) or swing through **Dun Strept** first (slower, but secures highland guides and muscle).
2. **Dun Strept:** Hesk Ironjaw refuses to lift a finger for Cintra until the party returns the **Marnadal torc** (or presents Kosk’s formal written apology and blood-price). In return, Hesk shows them the clan’s **carved standing stone** (confirming the fourth verse) and sends a seasoned highland war-band to pin down Choir guards outside Ostrel.
3. **Assengard:** Gruff smith **Dravin Ostrel** joins the party with the **Iron-Key** once shown proof of Anselm’s plan. He knows a forgotten postern—**the Weeping Gate** beneath the Old Grey Matron peak—that bypasses the main snow-choked portals.
4. **The Marnadal Stairs:** Blizzard whiteouts, swaying rope spans over glacial gorges, Choir rear-guard sharpshooters, and territorial **frost trolls** hunting along the ice-bridges.
5. **Wyvern’s Roost:** The wyvern from Tigg nests near the upper ridge. She is protecting her clutch after Choir scouts stole two eggs to use as alchemical reagents. Slay her, or return her stolen eggs and she carries the party across a collapsed chasm to the upper gate.
6. **Karak Ostrel (Dungeon):** Towering, frozen dwarven halls—**the Forge of Echoes**, **the Drowned Library**, and **the Bridge of Nine Anvils**. Pressure-plate rune traps, Choir zealots, and **automated dwarven iron-wardens** waking up as vault pressure drops.
7. **Finale — The Lock Chamber (Solstice Dawn):** Inside the cavernous control vault around the **Black Iron Throne**, Anselm holds a knife to Aldric’s throat, forcing the boy to chant the Pact backward as the first rays of solstice dawn strike the mirror-shafts.
   * **Table Mechanic:** Place **four lit candles** on the table. Each round/beat Anselm forces another reversed verse, blow out one candle—great bronze gears grind, steam vents burst, and the vault door inches open.
   * **Victory:** Break through the Choir bodyguards, free Aldric, and defend him while he speaks **all four true verses** (wearing the Crown) before the fourth candle goes out.
   * **Escalation:** If three candles go out, Anselm inhales the venting alchemical fumes and fights as a monstrous **Ash-Bound Zealot**—wreathed in choking black soot, blistering heat, and jagged obsidian-like slag.

## 8. The Lion’s Oath (Table Handout)

### The True Oath (Locks the Vaults)
> *"By iron forged in mountain deep,*
> *By lion’s blood the watch we keep.*
> *Let bolt and bar hold fast the night,*
> *Till nineteen winters turn to light."*

### The Reversed Chant (Breaks the Seals)
> *"From light to dark the winters turn,*
> *Let bar and bolt to ashes burn.*
> *By lion’s blood the gates unbind,*
> *Wake what the mountain left behind."*

## 9. Campaign Endings

| Ending | Condition | Aftermath & Sequel Hooks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Lion Holds** | Queen saved + True Oath spoken | Ysolde recovers slowly; the vaults hold for another 19 years. Aldric emerges hardened beyond his years. The party is granted titles, and a fragile peace begins with Strept. |
| **The Boy King** | Queen dies + True Oath spoken | The vaults are sealed, but Aldric takes the throne at 12. Kosk and Varn clash over the regency while Duke Raimund declares Attre independent. **Sequel:** Cintran civil war. |
| **The Shattered Lock** | Reversed Oath finished; Anselm killed | The vault doors buckle open partially—ancient dwarven war-machines, blight-casks, and Choir fanatics scatter across the borderlands. **Sequel:** Hunting rogue weapons before Sodden claims them. |
| **Ashes & Iron** | Reversed Oath finished; Anselm lives | Karak Ostrel ruptures; soot-choked constructs and fanatics pour into the valley. Cintra faces ruin unless the party brokers a desperate alliance with Strept and Sodden to hold the passes. |
| **The Bitter Bargain** | Party seizes the Vault’s power first | A ruthless Realpolitik ending: the party or Crown uses Ostrel’s terrifying arsenal to crush the Choir, Attre, and Sodden—turning Cintra into a feared military autocracy. |
